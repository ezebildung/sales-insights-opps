Insights

Competitor Twilio (SMS/OTP) and Brevo (email) are incumbent tools OneSignal is being evaluated against
Objection Enterprise vendor-homologation and Central Bank approval can take up to a year before go-live
Use Case Fintech leads want journeys for loan reminders, KYC re-engagement, and tag-based segmentation
Doubt Leads repeatedly confuse tags vs. events and are unsure which to use for segmentation
Doubt Enterprise buyers ask about IP whitelisting and SSO (Azure Entra ID) before approving OneSignal

Opportunities & Learnings

Compliance questions like SSO and IP whitelisting tend to surface late in enterprise conversations
Pricing and usage numbers are often estimated live on calls instead of prepared beforehand
Tag vs. event terminology confuses leads repeatedly and needs a clearer standard explanation
Demo environments sometimes hit account limits (e.g. segment caps) mid-demo, causing friction
Long generic product walkthroughs can delay addressing the client's specific questions

Product Gaps

No IP whitelisting or SSO (e.g. Azure Entra ID) support for account access; API-key auth only
No native WhatsApp channel; requires webhooks and the Meta API
No standalone WhatsApp send type; must create then disable a Journey for one-off sends
Date-type event properties require manual string-to-Unix-timestamp conversion for integration

Opportunities & Learnings

Pricing calls often end without a clear counter-anchor, leaving the negotiation open-ended
Feature-usage needs surface after a high quote is presented, instead of shaping it upfront
Regulated-industry reference requests are handled reactively instead of pre-arranged in advance
Deep compliance questions (DRP, rollback, data residency) catch reps without ready answers
Checking a prospect's billing/usage visibility beforehand could sharpen the value pitch

Product Gaps

No documented Disaster Recovery Plan / multi-region failover story for OTP-critical customers
No documented rollback process for individual API-triggered sends like OTP, unlike bulk campaigns
Org-level Admin role is separate from app-level Admin, hiding billing/usage from app-level admins
No UI-level way to audit which data tags exist or how/why they were set
No PII/data-classification matrix deliverable available for compliance-driven enterprise buyers
